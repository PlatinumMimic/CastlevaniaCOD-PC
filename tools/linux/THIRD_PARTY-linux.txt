FFmpeg 7.1.2 (https://ffmpeg.org), in lib/

The HD movies' decoding, unmodified, linked dynamically: libavformat.so.61, libavcodec.so.61, libavutil.so.59 and
libswscale.so.8 may be replaced by any compatible build of them. Built under the LGPL alone (no GPL or nonfree
part), configured with --disable-everything --enable-decoder=h264,hevc --enable-parser=h264,hevc
--enable-demuxer=mov --enable-protocol=file --enable-swscale --enable-shared --disable-autodetect. Its source:
https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.2.tar.xz

hope that it will be useful, but WITHOUT ANY WARRANTY; without even the implied warranty of MERCHANTABILITY or
FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.
